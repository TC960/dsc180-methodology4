**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

That LLMs actually possess nuanced emotion. I didn't think this was possible, since emotions feel so intangible. I was also surprised by how easy it seems to be to steer models using techniques that are easy to understand.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

Comparing how LLMs represent emotions with how the human brain does. I would extract emotion directions from a model's internal activations, then use representational similarity analysis to compare them with fMRI recordings of people reading or listening to the same emotional text, using a public fMRI dataset. The question is whether emotions that sit close together inside the model (for example, fear and anxiety) also sit close together in the brain.

**What is a potential change you'd make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?**

Right now I am thinking about the problem in a very mathematical way. A change I would make is to also check how our results compare with neuroscience, for example whether the emotion representations we find in the model line up with what is known about how the brain represents emotion.
